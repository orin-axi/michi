**(b) Verdict: genuinely complementary, not competing** — but with one important caveat: headroom has already partially reinvented TOON's core idea for its compression path, so michi's win here is standardization/protocol-conformance, not introducing a novel technique.

- **Compression technique**: `crates/headroom-core/src/transforms/smart_crusher/` is a statistical/semantic sampler (entropy, sequential-ID detection, rare-status preservation, structural-outlier detection, k-split row sampling) — genuinely different from TOON, which is a pure rendering grammar with no sampling logic. `json_minifier.rs` is separately just whitespace-stripping. Neither overlaps with TOON.

So headroom's authors already know about TOON, already judged it useful, and already built a bespoke non-conformant clone (CSV-escaped rows instead of TOON's actual grammar) rather than depending on a TOON implementation.
